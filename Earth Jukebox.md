# Earth Jukebox

Each map is played as music, left to right. Higher value, higher pitch. West is in your left ear, east in your right. Rougher sound means more variation in that slice of the map.

Keyboard shortcuts and about the data

Space play or pause · ← → move playhead · ↑ ↓ change year · 1 2 3 pick track.

This prototype uses synthetic grids shaped like NASA Earth Information Center datasets, so the sounds are illustrative, not measurements. To use real data, replace `makeGrid()` with a function that returns a 72×36 array for the chosen year.
